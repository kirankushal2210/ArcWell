Try it

  • Tap Get Started, pick a role, and answer each question. Next waits for an answer; Skip doesn't.
  • Drag the wheel pickers, or flick them. Mouse wheel and arrow keys work too.
  • Swipe the phone in the welcome hero, or tap Training · Meals · Recovery.
  • Drag from the left edge of the phone to go back, or press Esc.

Motion spec

InteractionBehaviourSpring
Next and BackPush from the right; the old screen slides 30% left and dims. Back retraces the same path.damping 1.0 · response 0.42 s
Swipe from the left edgeFollows the finger 1:1. On release the velocity decides back or stay, and is handed to the spring, so there's no seam.same spring, initial velocity from the finger
Wheel pickersDrag 1:1 with rubber-banding past the ends. A flick is projected with Apple's deceleration (0.998), then snaps to a value, with a light tick per value.damping 1.0 · response 0.35 s
Welcome heroSwipe the phone or tap its switcher. The black thumb tracks the finger; a flick picks the page it's heading for.damping 1.0, or 0.85 after a flick
Role sheetRises without bounce. Drag down to dismiss; a flick dismisses even from a short drag. Scrim dims, never blurs.response 0.3 s · damping 1.0, or 0.8 after a flick
TapsHighlight on press, commit on release, cancel if the finger moves 10 px. Buttons scale to 97% while pressed.120 ms (duration-micro)
Calorie resultRing fills and the number counts up once. No confetti.damping 1.0 · response 0.6 s
Reduced motionSlides become cross-fades; press scaling is off.prefers-reduced-motion

Springs are set by damping ratio and response, as in Apple's Designing Fluid Interfaces, so any animation can be grabbed mid-flight and reversed from where it is. In the app these map to Reanimated springs and react-native-gesture-handler, with haptics from expo-haptics.
