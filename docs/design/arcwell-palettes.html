Arcwell · Liquid Glass · palette options

Five palettes. Same screen.

Everything else stays the same: true black, glass controls, the action dock, the tab bar and the + in the same places. Only the colours change. Each option passes contrast on black.

My pick: Jewel, or Volt if you want it bolder.

Jewel looks the most premium and the most Apple: four distinct gem colours that read clearly on black and stay pleasant to look at for months. Volt is the boldest and most memorable, a single signature colour like the big sports brands use, but the three pillars are told apart by icon and label rather than colour.
