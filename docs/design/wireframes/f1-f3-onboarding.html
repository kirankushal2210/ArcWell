F1 · Entry and account

Splash → Welcome → Sign in / Sign up → Consent → Onboarding (athlete) or Coach onboarding (F2)

F1 · Athlete onboarding · About you (D52)

Five sections on one progress bar: About you → Your goal → Training → Recovery → Nutrition → Your plan. One question per screen · Back always available · answers editable later in Profile (AC-07). Mockups: hifi-mockups/onboarding-flow.html.

F1 · Athlete onboarding · Your goal

What you want and how you live: the rest of the calorie maths.

F1 · Athlete onboarding · Training

Experience, place, schedule and limits: everything the starter workouts need.

F1 · Athlete onboarding · Recovery

Three light questions, all skippable. Free athletes have no Recovery tab (D2b): answers still space out hard sessions and carry over to Recovery on Plus or with a coach.

F1 · Athlete onboarding · Nutrition

Diet type and meal rhythm. The last question ends with "Finish", because a coached client has no plan yet.

F1 · Athlete onboarding · Your plan

Free athletes: AO-18 → AO-19 → AO-20 → AO-21. Coached athletes skip to AO-21b (the coach sets targets and plans).

F3 · Joining through a coach's invite link

Coach shares link on WhatsApp → client taps → Play Store (if not installed) → app opens on invite → sign up (role skipped) → consent without AI (F1-05b) → onboarding AO-01 to AO-17 → AO-21b
