Arcwell — Wireframes: F2 Coach onboarding

Goal: a coach goes from download to their first client joined in under 10 minutes, without needing a call or a tutorial. Only the profile is required; services, templates and invites can be done now or later from a setup checklist. Coaches are the paying customer, so this flow sells the time Arcwell saves them versus WhatsApp and Excel.
