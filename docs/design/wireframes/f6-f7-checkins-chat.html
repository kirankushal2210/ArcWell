Arcwell — Wireframes: F6 Weekly check-ins, F7 Chat

Check-ins replace "send me your photos and weight on WhatsApp every Sunday". Goals: the athlete finishes in under 3 minutes (most numbers are filled in from their logs); the coach reviews one client in under 2 minutes with everything side by side. Chat replaces WhatsApp for coaching conversations, with workouts and check-ins linked in context. Patterns adapted from Kahunas (check-in queue), TrueCoach and Linear (triage inbox).

Reminder (day set by coach) → body measurements → photos → how the week went → review → submit → coach feedback

Check-ins tab → inbox (overdue · to review · reviewed) → review one client → write feedback → send → next client

F7 · Chat

Athlete ↔ coach, in real time · text, photos, PDFs · workouts and check-ins linked in context · works on poor connections

Open questions from these wireframes

  • F6-04: Confirm the fixed check-in questions (energy, hunger, stress, digestion, wins, struggles). Allow coaches to add custom questions in v1.1?
  • F6-09: Saved replies for coaches — v1 or v1.1? Recommendation: v1.1.
  • F7-01: With video out of v1, should athletes be able to share form-check clips as a link (e.g. YouTube unlisted / Google Drive)? Recommendation: allow links in v1; native video in v2.
  • F7-04: Can coaches chat with leads (people who enquired but aren't clients yet) inside Arcwell, or does that stay on Instagram/WhatsApp in v1?
  • Not designed yet: voice notes (common in Indian coaching on WhatsApp), message search, coach "office hours" / auto-reply. Recommendation: voice notes in v1.1 — they're expected, but add storage and moderation cost.
