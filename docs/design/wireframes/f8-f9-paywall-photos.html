Arcwell — Wireframes: F8 Paywall & subscription, F9 Transformation photos

F8: an honest paywall, shown only when a free athlete reaches for a paid feature — never during onboarding, never to coached athletes. Clear INR prices, a trial timeline with a reminder, easy cancel. Pattern adapted from Strava's honest paywall (see references.md). F9: transformation photos belong to the athlete. A coach can only request; the athlete approves each photo, can blur their face, and can withdraw at any time (DPDP).
