Arcwell — Wireframes: F5 Food logging

Goals: log an Indian meal in under 30 seconds, in portions people actually think in (katori, roti, piece), with honest numbers. Free users get calories only; paid and coached users get full macros. AI photo logging is paid-only and always ends with a human confirm step. Patterns adapted from MyFitnessPal, HealthifyMe Snap and MacroFactor (see competitor-research.md).

Open questions from these wireframes

  • F5-02 / F5-14: Name of the paid tier — "Arcwell Plus" is a placeholder.
  • F5-05: Allow user-submitted foods to be shared with everyone (crowd database, like MyFitnessPal), or keep only verified + personal foods? Recommendation: verified + personal only in v1 — crowd data is the main accuracy complaint about MyFitnessPal.
  • F5-06: Confirm standard gram weights for Indian units (1 katori = 150 g? 1 phulka = 30 g?). Needs a nutrition reviewer.
  • F5-08: Recipe builder (build a dish from ingredients) — v1 or v1.1?
  • Not designed yet: water tracking, copying yesterday's meals, barcode scanning (out of v1). Recommendation: add "Copy from yesterday" in v1 — very cheap, very useful for repetitive Indian diets.
