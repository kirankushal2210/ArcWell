# Competitor Research

Researched 2026-11-10. Prices change often. Each figure gives its source and, where known, the date it was checked. Many third-party pricing pages are run by competing coach platforms (Coachway, FitBudd, QuickCoach, NutriScan, Nutrola), so read them with that bias in mind. "Not confirmed" means no reliable current source was found.

## 1. Landscape summary

Western coach platforms (Trainerize, TrueCoach, Everfit, FITR, Kahunas, Hevy Coach) are mature, priced in USD/GBP by client count, and strongest on training. Nutrition tends to be weak or a paid add-on, and Indian food data and units are missing. Hevy Coach has the best logging UX but no nutrition and no check-ins. FitBudd is Indian-founded but bills in USD and leads with a white-label app. In India, consumer players (HealthifyMe, Fittr, Cult.fit) sell coaching *to consumers*. None of them is a tool for an independent coach to run their own clients. Indian Instagram coaches therefore still run on WhatsApp, Sheets, PDFs and Google Forms. The only explicitly India-first coach SaaS found, TrainZilla (₹999/mo), is new and sales-focused. Athlete trackers set the UX bar: Hevy and Strong for logging, Cal AI and HealthifyMe Snap for photo logging, Whoop for recovery. They also show the backlash risks: MyFitnessPal's paywalls and Cal AI's accuracy and billing complaints.

## 3. Competitors

**Trainerize.** Market default for PTs and gyms (ABC Fitness). *Strengths:* complete feature set, branded app. *Weaknesses:* add-ons pile up (meal plans $20–45, payments $10, video $10, branded app $169 setup). Reviews cite crashes, slowness on older Android phones and poor support (Trustpilot 3.5/5). *Borrow:* compliance flags on the client list. *Avoid:* paid add-ons, a weak Android app.

**TrueCoach.** Programming-first tool for strength coaches. *Strengths:* fast workout builder, payments included, clients free. *Weaknesses:* nutrition is only a MyFitnessPal link. *Borrow:* simple tiers by client count. *Avoid:* outsourcing nutrition.

**Everfit.** Feature-rich, with a free tier up to 5 clients. *Strengths:* Autoflow automation. *Weaknesses:* G2 reviewers flag weak nutrition; meal plans, automation and payments all cost extra. *Borrow:* a free starter tier. *Avoid:* selling nutrition as an upsell.

**FITR.** Since May 2026, every price band includes the full platform; only the client cap differs. *Strengths:* transparent pricing, auto-upgrade instead of blocking signups. *Weaknesses:* 5.99% fee on processed revenue. *Borrow:* the same features at every tier, plus auto-upgrade. *Avoid:* taking a cut of coach revenue.

**Kahunas.** Popular with physique coaches. *Strengths:* structured check-ins, habits, 0% payment commission, Trustpilot 4.8. *Weaknesses:* meal plans built by hand; features and branded app (200+ clients) gated by roster size. *Borrow:* a workflow built around check-ins. *Avoid:* gating core features behind client counts.

**Hevy Coach.** Coach layer on the Hevy tracker; clients get Hevy Pro free. *Strengths:* best logging UX, cheap. *Weaknesses:* no nutrition, check-ins, payments or branding. *Borrow:* "the coach pays, the client gets a premium tracker free" (matches Arcwell's model). *Avoid:* training-only scope.

**FitBudd.** Gurugram-founded, leads with a branded app. *Strengths:* white-label app, website, payments. *Weaknesses:* USD billing; white-label costs $149/mo plus store fees. *Borrow:* a coach storefront. *Avoid:* USD-only pricing.

**TrainZilla (added).** Closest India-first coach SaaS: rupee pricing, Instagram-bio links, WhatsApp sales scripts. *Weaknesses:* focused on sales; Indian food data and logging depth not confirmed. *Borrow:* the ₹999 price anchor. *Avoid:* leading with lead-gen before the coaching tools work well.

**Fittr.** India's largest fitness community and coaching marketplace (700+ coaches claimed), plus the HART ring and diagnostics. *Strengths:* trust, transformation culture. *Weaknesses:* coaches work under Fittr's brand; reviews mention UI issues; no public prices. *Borrow:* transformation storytelling. *Avoid:* owning the coach's client relationship.

**HealthifyMe.** Mass-market Indian calorie app (Snap photo logging, Ria AI). *Strengths:* deepest regional Indian food database and Indian units. *Weaknesses:* Trustpilot 2.9/5, with complaints about coach turnover, generic plans and refunds. *Borrow:* regional dish coverage, photo logging tuned for Indian plates. *Avoid:* inconsistent human-coach upsells.

**Cult.fit.** Gyms plus a content app (cultpass ELITE/PRO/PLAY/HOME). *Weaknesses:* aggressive sales calls; buffering and crashes (Quash release-health score 28/100, Sep 2026). *Borrow:* short guided no-equipment workouts. *Avoid:* pushy sales, Android instability.

**Hevy.** The logging benchmark: previous values pre-filled, auto rest timer, supersets, Strong CSV import, Wear OS, social feed that can be hidden. *Borrow:* nearly all of the logging UX.

**Strong.** Minimal logger with unlimited free logs and no ads (3-template cap); its Android app trails iOS. *Borrow:* a calm free tier. *Avoid:* second-class Android.

**MyFitnessPal.** Sentiment fell after barcode scanning moved behind the paywall and a 2026 redesign added taps. Indian entries are crowdsourced and logged in grams or cups, not katori or roti. *Borrow:* recent foods and quick-add. *Avoid:* paywalling basic logging.

**Cal AI.** Photo-first calorie app, reportedly acquired by MyFitnessPal in 2026 (not confirmed). Accuracy drops on mixed dishes such as thalis, and users complain about dynamic pricing and refunds. *Borrow:* photo, then edit, then confirm. *Avoid:* presenting estimates as exact.

**Whoop.** Recovery benchmark: daily Recovery % (HRV, resting HR, sleep), Strain 0–21, Sleep Coach. Main complaint is cost (₹21,990+/yr). *Borrow:* one readiness number with a plain-language reason. *Avoid:* requiring hardware. Arcwell v1 recovery must come from self-reported sleep, soreness, stress and energy.

## 4. Gaps Arcwell can own

1. **Coach SaaS built for Indian coaches.** No established platform prices in INR, bills via UPI/Razorpay, or targets Instagram coaches running on WhatsApp. TrainZilla is the only India-first entrant and is early. Target ₹999–2,499/mo.
2. **Indian nutrition inside a coach tool.** HealthifyMe has the Indian food data but no coach CRM. Coach platforms have CRMs but Western food data. IFCT data, regional dishes, katori/roti units, veg/Jain/eggetarian filters and coach meal plans in one product is unclaimed.
3. **A real WhatsApp replacement.** Chat with attachments, check-ins with a set due day, overdue flags and plan delivery on one screen. Chat is secondary in Western tools but central for Indian coaches.
4. **Everything included, only the coach pays.** Others charge extra for meal plans and payments (Trainerize, Everfit) or take a revenue share (FITR 5.99%). Include nutrition, check-ins and chat at every tier; clients pay ₹0.
5. **The coach owns the client.** Fittr, HealthifyMe and Cult.fit keep the client relationship. Arcwell leaves it with the coach (invite links, a coach profile with athlete-approved transformation photos).
6. **Training, nutrition and recovery in one app,** including a recovery check-in that needs no wearable. No coach tool offers this.
7. **Android-first quality.** Trainerize, Strong and Cult.fit all draw Android complaints, and India is mostly Android.
8. **An honest AI tier:** cheap, sends serious goals to human coaches, and shows estimates the user can edit. Avoids both Cal AI's backlash and HealthifyMe's coach upsell.

## 5. UX patterns worth adopting

- **Hevy set logging:** previous weight × reps pre-filled inline, tap to complete a set, rest timer starts on its own, superset and drop-set markers, PR badges at the end.
- **Strong's free tier:** unlimited logging, no ads. Charge for advanced features, never the core action.
- **Kahunas/Trainerize check-ins:** fixed weekly form (weight, photos, adherence ratings, notes) reviewed from a due/overdue queue.
- **Client list as triage:** last active, compliance and flags on each row, so the coach can see who needs attention today.
- **FITR auto-upgrade:** when a coach exceeds the plan limit, move them up a tier; never block a new client from joining.
- **Snap/Cal AI photo logging:** photo, detected items, adjust portions in Indian units, then a mandatory confirm step.
- **Recent foods and "same as yesterday"** (MyFitnessPal): Indian diets repeat often, so this saves the most time.
- **Whoop readiness:** one score, a colour band, a one-line reason, a suggested action.
- **Hevy Coach model:** coached clients get the premium tracker free.
- **Data portability:** CSV export plus Strong/Hevy import to lower switching cost.
