Stone, with a new Training colour.

Everything you liked stays: the charcoal, sage for Nutrition, slate blue for Recovery, soft white actions, rose for anything new, the slim buttons and the dense Today. Only Training changes. Clay is first for comparison.

My pick: Honey.

Honey keeps the warmth you liked in Clay but with more life, and it is the easiest of the four to tell apart from sage, slate blue and rose at a glance. If you want Training to feel hotter, Persimmon; if you want Stone cooler and more modern, Lagoon.
