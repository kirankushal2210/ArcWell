The clutter rules these screens follow

Only what you chose

Every add-on is a module the athlete turns on. Off means no trace anywhere.

1 hero + 3 tiles

Today never shows more. Anything else waits in one "Later today" row.

Habits keep their hours

Each one appears only in its window, worked out from your own wake time and bedtime, then leaves.

Five habits at most

New athletes start with three, picked from their onboarding answers.

Two nudges a day

At most, chosen by the athlete. Quiet from bedtime to wake time.

No new tabs

Habits live inside Today, Recovery and Nutrition. Race is a 4th section tab only for race athletes.

No guilt

Weekly consistency, not streaks. A missed habit is never red.
