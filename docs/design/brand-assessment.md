# Arcwell — Brand and Product Assessment

Pre-design assessment, 2026-11-10. Answers come from the founder interview (see `docs/SPEC.md`). Items marked **ASSUMPTION** were not stated by the founder and need confirming.

## 1. What the brand is about

**Why Arcwell exists.** Online fitness coaching in India is booming on Instagram, but much of it is low-effort: copy-pasted ChatGPT plans, no tailoring, clients managed over WhatsApp and Excel. Good coaches drown in admin; clients pay separately for training, nutrition and recovery apps. Arcwell gives good coaches one professional platform, and gives people who can't afford a coach a credible, cheaper AI alternative.

**Mission (draft):** Make good coaching easier to deliver and harder to fake.

**What Arcwell stands for**

| Value | Means in practice |
|---|---|
| Tailored, not templated | Plans are built for the person: goals, limits, equipment, diet type. AI plans only use a reviewed exercise library. |
| Respect for the coach | We are the coach's tool, never their competitor. Coached clients never see AI. |
| Honesty | Real numbers, no fake precision, no shaming; clear about what AI can and can't do. |
| Consistency over intensity | Daily habits and steady progress, not hype or streak pressure. |
| Privacy | Health data and photos belong to the athlete; consent is explicit and reversible. |

**Personality:** calm, competent, trustworthy — like a respected senior coach. Not loud, not "bro", not gamified.

**Is / is not**

| Arcwell is | Arcwell is not |
|---|---|
| Professional, clear, quietly confident | Neon, aggressive, "beast mode" |
| Warm and encouraging | Preachy or shaming |
| Evidence-minded | Pseudo-science or miracle claims |
| Indian-first (foods, units, prices) | A US app with Indian food bolted on |

**Promise / tagline (draft):** "Training, nutrition and recovery, coached as one."

**Positioning statement (draft):** For online fitness coaches in India who run clients on WhatsApp and spreadsheets, Arcwell is the all-in-one coaching platform that brings training, nutrition, recovery, check-ins and chat into one app their clients use free. Unlike generic trackers or Western coaching tools, Arcwell is built for Indian food, Indian prices and the way Indian coaches actually work.

## 2. What the mobile app needs to achieve

### Business goals (v1)

1. **Win coaches.** Get 3–5 beta coaches running *all* their clients on Arcwell for 2–4 weeks, then convert them to paying.
2. **Replace WhatsApp + Excel.** A coach should be able to stop using spreadsheets for plans, logs and check-ins entirely.
3. **Earn athlete subscriptions.** Convert free athletes to the paid AI tier at natural moments (wanting macros, an AI plan, recovery).
4. **Build trust.** Zero data leaks between coaches; clear consent; reliable logging (no lost workouts or chats).

### Experience goals

| For | The app must feel… | Measured by (ASSUMPTION — targets to confirm) |
|---|---|---|
| Coach | Faster than WhatsApp + Excel | Time to onboard a client < 5 min; check-in review < 2 min per client |
| Coached athlete | Effortless to follow the coach | Workout set logged in ≤ 2 taps; daily logging adherence |
| Free athlete | Useful from day one | Logs a first workout or meal in the first session |
| Paid athlete | Worth paying for | Free → paid conversion; 30-day retention of paid users |

### Success metrics (v1) — ASSUMPTION

- Coaches: weekly active coaches, clients per coach, % of check-ins reviewed within 48 h, coach retention after beta.
- Athletes: day-7 and day-30 retention, workouts and meals logged per week, free → paid conversion.
- Quality: crash-free sessions > 99.5%, zero cross-coach data incidents.

## 3. What the user is actually supposed to do

### Personas

**Coach — "Joseph", 29, Instagram online coach (Bengaluru).** 40 clients, coaches over WhatsApp, plans in Excel/PDF, collects check-ins as photos in chats. Wants to look professional, save hours each week, and retain clients. Pain: chasing check-ins, scattered data, copy-pasting plans.

**Coached athlete — "Priya", 26, Joseph's client (Pune).** Paying for coaching to lose fat and get stronger. Wants to know exactly what to do today and to stay accountable. Pain: plans buried in chat, forgetting what she lifted last week, logging food is tedious with Indian meals.

**Free / paid athlete — "Sanjeev", 31, self-directed gym-goer (Hyderabad).** Can't justify ₹5–10k/month on a coach. Wants a sensible plan and calorie guidance for Indian food. Upgrades if the AI plan and macros feel genuinely tailored.

### Jobs to be done

| Who | When… | I want to… | So I can… |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coach | a new client signs up | send one link that sets everything up | start coaching without admin |
| Coach | it's check-in day | see who's submitted, who's late, and what changed | give useful feedback fast |
| Coach | I write a programme | reuse and customise templates | stop rebuilding plans from scratch |
| Coached athlete | I open the app | see today's workout, meals and tasks | just follow the plan |
| Coached athlete | I'm mid-workout | log sets in seconds with last week's numbers shown | progress without thinking |
| Any athlete | I eat a meal | log Indian food in Indian portions quickly | track calories/macros accurately |
| Paid athlete | I have no coach | get a plan that fits my goals, time and equipment | train sensibly without paying a coach |
| Athlete | I'm proud of my progress | approve (or refuse) my coach sharing my photos | stay in control of my images |

### Core tasks per role (what the app must make easy)

- **Coach:** invite clients · build/assign plans · review check-ins and reply · chat · track adherence · manage client status · manage leads and profile.
- **Coached athlete:** follow today's plan · log workouts · log food · submit weekly check-in · chat with coach · track progress.
- **Free athlete:** use starter templates · log workouts · log calories · upgrade when ready.
- **Paid athlete:** get and adjust AI plans · log with full macros and photo logging · track recovery and progress.
